River Outbreak

Rivers worldwide have suddenly been infested with zombie like horses. And have spread to towns and cities

Scientists have studied a bit from afar and have noticed three things that will make it easy to survive these Zombie horses. They're not that dangerous but still should be wary of being brought to any bodies of water.

  • Don't go near any water
  • Don't feed them
  • Be kind if ever in contact

Survival Kits:

A Net

A large net you can use to keep your target down, giving you time to run.

A flower

You could give it to an infected zombie horse... I guess? Could establish a friendship.

A sadle and reins

You could possibly ride the horse and look pretty dang cool. Just steer clear of any ponds or rivers.

There are safe zones, normally pretty high up where the horses can't go. Unless there is an elevator... Then they'll somehow get up

But other than that you should be safe.
